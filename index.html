03 / Profile

My journey into
technology.

I became interested in programming at around the age of 11. At that time, I didn’t have the opportunity to study properly or practice consistently, so eventually I had to put it aside for a while.

Around the age of 15, I returned to programming with a more conscious approach and began actively developing my skills in the IT field. I started with web development, focusing on building websites and learning the basics of frontend development. Later, I decided to explore cybersecurity, as it seemed like a more complex and structured field. During that period, I studied Linux, Bash, and networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP.

Over time, I realized that I wanted to focus on web development and make up for lost time. Currently, I continue to grow in this area, and in the future, I plan to learn Java, Node.js, and React to build more advanced and modern projects.
